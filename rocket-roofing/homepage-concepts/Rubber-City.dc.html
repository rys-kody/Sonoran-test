What a Northeast Ohio year does to a roof.

A roof built for Phoenix fails here. These are the four things we design every Rocket roof to survive.

January

Ice dams

Heat escapes, snow melts, refreezes at the edge, and water backs up under the shingles.

Our fix: ice and water shield at every eave, plus balanced attic ventilation.

February

Freeze and thaw

Water gets into small gaps, freezes, expands, and opens them wider every week.

Our fix: new flashing and pipe boots on every replacement, never reused.

March

Heavy, wet snow

Weeks of load find every soft spot in an aging roof deck.

Our fix: we walk and check the full deck after tear-off and photograph what we replace.

July

Wind and hail

Summer storms lift tabs, crease shingles, and strip granules.

Our fix: manufacturer high-wind nailing, with impact-rated shingles available.
