Your first 48 hours after a storm.

What you do now decides how your claim goes later.

  1. 01
    Photograph everything from the ground.

    Date-stamped photos of the yard, gutters, siding, and any debris. Do not climb the roof.

  2. 02
    Stop the water inside.

    Buckets, towels, move valuables. Then call us.

  3. 03
    Keep every receipt.

    Emergency repairs and supplies are often part of the claim.

  4. 04
    Do not sign anything at the door.

    Out-of-town crews follow storms. Ask for a local address and look it up.

  5. 05
    Get an inspection before you file.

    So the claim you file is backed by evidence, and only filed when it should be.
